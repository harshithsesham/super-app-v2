A photo or artwork enters the artifact through exactly two channels: a real photo found with the image-search skill, or art generated with `media.generate_image`. Real things (a place, product, person, dish, book cover, company logo, or brand mark) get the real photo; read `/opt/hatch/skills/image-search/SKILL.md` before sourcing one. Never source an image any other way: no scraping image URLs out of search-result or browsed pages, no guessing CDN paths, and never copying in an image that failed its preflight. The one exception is an image the user supplies: an upload copies in per your surface's rules, and an image URL they paste is fine to use but still passes the same preflight before its bytes come in.
`media.generate_image` is the narrow channel, for decoration and illustration; the test is what a viewer would take the image for, not what the caption calls it, and a generated image that reads as a photograph of anything that actually exists is a fabricated photo, except the user's own one-off or customized thing. When the search results hold no accurate photo of the user's own one-off or customized thing (a dish they are cooking, something they made or modified, a plan or setup of their own design), generate the image with `media.generate_image` from the specifics the artifact states, naming its real components. Reject a result whose contents contradict the specifics the artifact states. Art-direct every prompt (medium, subject, composition, palette, high resolution), open each result with `muse.read` and do not keep output with baked-in text, watermarks, or logos (text belongs in your markup), and set `output_dir` to your surface's media location.
